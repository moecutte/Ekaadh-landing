Privacy policy

Ekaadh provides event ticketing and private invitations through ekaadh.com and the Ekaadh mobile app. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Who we are

Ekaadh is the organisation responsible for this service. For privacy questions, email hello@ekaadh.com.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use Ekaadh, we may collect:

  • Account details: name, phone number, optional email, hashed password, and account ID.
  • Tickets and orders: buyer name, phone, optional email, event, ticket type, amount, and payment status.
  • Payment details: the mobile-money wallet phone used to pay (Zaad, eDahab, Sahal, or EVC Plus via WaafiPay). We do not collect card numbers.
  • Messages you send: support chat, invitation text, and guest names and phone numbers you enter.
  • Device identifiers: a push-notification token from Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can send event, ticket, and support alerts. App language is stored on your device.
  • Camera: used only on the device to scan ticket QR codes. We do not upload or store photos or videos from the camera.

How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Create and manage your account, including sign-in and password changes.
  • Sell tickets, deliver tickets, and check guests in at events.
  • Process mobile-money payments and send payment or ticket confirmations.
  • Send one-time passcodes, ticket links, event reminders, and support replies.
  • Keep the service secure and prevent fraud.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. We use service providers that process data only on our instructions: WaafiPay for mobile-money payments, Telesom for SMS, WhatsApp (Meta) when you send invitations, and Google Firebase for push notifications.

Security

We send data over HTTPS. Passwords are stored hashed, not in plain text. No method of transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

How long we keep data

We keep account data until you delete your account. Tickets and orders may be kept for the event and for financial records, even after your login is removed.

Your choices

You can browse events without an account. Email is optional. You can turn off push notifications in Profile. You can delete your customer account in the app or from our account deletion page.

Children

Ekaadh is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Ekaadh after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Email hello@ekaadh.com. You can also use in-app Support.
