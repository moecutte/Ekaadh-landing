Account

Delete your Ekaadh account

You can delete your customer account at any time. Use the app if you still have it installed, or email us if you have uninstalled it. Organizer and staff accounts cannot be deleted from the app — email us instead.

Delete from the Ekaadh app

This is the fastest option if the app is still on your phone.

  1. Open the Ekaadh app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm. Deletion is immediate.

Request deletion by email

Use this if you uninstalled the app, cannot sign in, or have an organizer/staff account.

Send a message to:

hello@ekaadh.com

Include:

  • The name on the account
  • The phone number used to sign in or buy tickets
  • The email on the account, if you added one

We process requests within 7 days after we can verify the account.

What is deleted

We remove your login, name, phone, email, password, push tokens, and in-app notifications. You will not be able to sign in again with that account.

Tickets and orders you already paid for stay valid for the event and for our financial records. Those records are no longer linked to an active login.
